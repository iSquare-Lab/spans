How to play

  1. Each island's number is how many bridges it needs.
  2. Bridges run straight across or up and down, from one island to the next one it can see.
  3. Two islands share at most two bridges, and bridges never cross.
  4. When you are done, every island can reach every other one.

To build: drag from an island towards a neighbour, or tap the water between them. Again for a second bridge, a third time to remove them.

Islands turn green when they have the right number of bridges and red when they have too many.

Every puzzle has exactly one solution, reached by reasoning alone. Easy puzzles never need more than one step of thinking at a time; hard ones ask you to look further ahead.

The daily puzzles are the same for everyone and change at midnight. Your progress stays in this browser.
